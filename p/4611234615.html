Quote every install with the hair cost built in, get the consultation and consent form signed, and know when each client is due for a move-up: a complete hair extension forms kit with a working pricing calculator and client tracker, not just a few printable pages.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- Hair Extension Consultation & Consent Form (Word + PDF, 2 pages): client details, current hair and goals (length, density, texture, recent color or chemical services, past extensions, budget), stylist's recommended plan and quote (method: tape-in, K-tip / keratin bond, I-tip / micro bead, hand-tied or sewn weft, clip-in; length, grams / pieces / rows, shade match for roots, mids and ends, hair brand and batch, hair + install price, deposit, move-up schedule), simple yes / no client self-disclosure questions, patch test / test bond record or signed decline, 8 initialed acknowledgments (tension and natural hair, color match and results, maintenance, custom hair non-refundable once ordered, professional removal, policies agreed), consent and release, before / after photo consent, client and stylist signatures, yearly review / update log.
- Hair Extension Client Record & Color Match Card (Word + PDF, 2 pages): shade match table (roots, mid-lengths, ends, blend), natural hair, method, length, grams and placement notes, hair brand and batch, install and move-up history (method / amount, new or reused hair, time, price, next move-up), hair orders, deposits and balances, before / after photo log.
- Hair Extension Salon Policies (Word + PDF): 9 ready-to-edit policies for consultations, deposits and hair orders, patch tests / test bonds, arriving for your appointment, late arrivals and cancellations, maintenance and move-ups, hair reuse and returns, removal, payments and right to refuse, plus a blank page for your own aftercare instructions.
- Hair Extension Service Menu & Price List (Word + PDF): editable menu with times and prices for consultations, installs, move-ups, removal and add-ons, plus a hair price table by length.
- Hair Extension Pricing Calculator & Client Tracker (Google Sheets / Excel, 6 tabs):
* Service Menu: minutes, hair and product cost and your price for each install, move-up, removal and add-on; suggested price from your target hourly pay, overhead and card fees; what you keep and your real pay per hour; BELOW SUGGESTED flag
* Settings: target pay, monthly overhead, booked hours, card fees, deposit % and minimum, rounding, and after how many days your salon asks for a new patch test / test bond (your own policy)
* Clients: contact details, method / length / grams / shade notes, consent form date, patch test / test bond date or "not needed / waived" mark, visits, total spent, retail bought, next move-up due and rebook alerts (REBOOK NOW / DUE SOON / BOOKED); no health information stored
* Appointments: service and add-on looked up from your menu, discounts, deposits, refunds, status, retail sold, days since last visit, balances and alerts (CONSENT MISSING / PATCH TEST MISSING / BALANCE DUE / DEPOSIT DUE / SEND FORMS / PATCH TEST DUE / UPCOMING / UPDATE STATUS)
* Dashboard: service revenue, retail sales, tips, hair and product costs, card fees, what you keep, pay per hour worked, average ticket, no-show rate, deposits held, month-by-month and service-by-service tables, chart and a list of appointments that need attention
- START HERE guide (PDF)

HOW IT WORKS
1. Enter your target pay, overhead, card fees and patch test / test bond policy on Settings.
2. Add your services on the Service Menu tab with the hair cost for each install, fix anything flagged BELOW SUGGESTED, and copy your prices into the printable menu.
3. At the consultation, fill in the plan and quote, then have the client sign the Consultation & Consent Form; note the consent date and test date on Clients.
4. Log each booking and deposit; price, time and hair cost fill in automatically.
5. After each visit, record payment, tip and retail sales, and watch the alerts for missing consent forms or patch tests, balances, deposits and clients due for a move-up.
Example data (9 services, 14 sample clients, 32 appointments) shows how it all connects; delete it and type your own. Yellow cells = type here, green cells = calculated.
