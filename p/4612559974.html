Book the date, collect the retainer, set up without surprises and get paid in full: a complete photo booth contract kit with a working quote calculator and booking / retainer tracker, not just a one-page contract.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- Photo Booth Rental Agreement (Word + PDF, 2 pages): event details, package and price table (booth package, extra hours, scrapbook, backdrop, props, custom print design, travel, discount), non-refundable retainer, balance due date, 15 editable terms (services and booth time, retainer, balance and payment, extra time, venue space and power, weather and outdoor events, print design approval, photos and online gallery, guests and equipment damage, safety, cancellation and rescheduling, equipment problems, liability, events outside anyone's control, governing law), payment record and signatures.
- Photo Booth Event Planning Form (Word + PDF, 2 pages): client, event and venue details, load-in and parking, booth location, space and power, package choices, prints, backdrop, props, sharing options, print / overlay design details, booking summary, notes and photo permission.
- Photo Booth Setup Checklist & Event Day Run Sheet (Word + PDF, 2 pages): before-the-event checks, 16-item packing list, setup and test checklist, a timed run sheet for the day and a close-out record (actual times, extra time, sessions, prints used, gallery delivered).
- Print Design Approval & Incident Report (Word + PDF, 2 pages): proof checklist (names, date, hashtag, colors, logo, layout), client sign-off, change log, and an equipment damage and incident report with items affected and follow-up.
- Photo Booth Quote Calculator & Booking Tracker (Google Sheets / Excel, 6 tabs):
* Settings: attendant pay, setup + teardown hours, overhead, booth hours per month, cost per print, prints per hour, travel rules, target margin, card fees, minimum booking, retainer %, balance due days and design approval days
* Packages: your packages, extra hours and add-ons with booth hours, price and supplies cost, a suggested price from your costs and BELOW SUGGESTED flags
* Quotes: up to 4 items x quantity, prints yes / no, start time; booth hours, booth end time, hours on site, travel fee, total, retainer, estimated prints, cost, profit and margin with a LOW MARGIN flag
* Bookings: venue, contract date, retainer, balance payments, custom design approval and venue check; balance, balance due date, estimated profit, days to event and alerts (CONTRACT MISSING, RETAINER DUE, BALANCE OVERDUE, BALANCE DUE, UPDATE STATUS, DESIGN APPROVAL, VENUE CHECK, BALANCE DUE SOON, UPCOMING)
* Dashboard: quotes and acceptance rate, revenue booked, collected, balances to collect, overdue balances, estimated costs and profit, average booking, retainers kept from cancellations, month-by-month and event-type tables, chart and a list of bookings that need attention
- START HERE guide (PDF)

HOW IT WORKS
1. Enter your pay, costs, print cost, travel rules and retainer % on Settings, then your packages and add-ons on Packages.
2. Take the enquiry with the Event Planning Form and add a row to Quotes: the total, retainer and booth end time fill in.
3. When the client says yes, send the Rental Agreement and add the booking with the contract date and retainer.
4. Send the print design proof with the Design Approval form, confirm the venue, then use the Setup Checklist and Run Sheet on the day.
5. Check the Dashboard weekly for balances due, missing contracts, design approvals and venue checks.
Example data (25 quotes, 21 bookings) shows how it all connects; delete it and type your own. Yellow cells = type here, green cells = calculated.
