Selling your products to boutiques, gift shops or salons? Send buyers an order form that does the math and catches mistakes, then track every wholesale order until it's paid.

SMART ORDER FORM (Excel / Google Sheets)
• Products tab: SKU, product, wholesale price, MSRP, case pack, markup to MSRP
• Order form: pick a SKU, enter quantity - product name, price and line total fill in
• Flags any quantity that isn't a full case pack
• Checks your opening-order or reorder minimum and shows how much short the buyer is
• Free shipping above your threshold, flat fee below it
• Payment line with your Net terms due date (or "Prepay")
• Print it or save as PDF to send to buyers

WHOLESALE ORDER TRACKER (Excel / Google Sheets)
• Orders log (500 rows): order #, date, store, buyer, total, status (Quote / Confirmed / Shipped / Cancelled), ship date
• Due date calculates from your payment terms; payment status shows Paid, Due, Not shipped or OVERDUE (red)
• Dashboard: wholesale revenue, paid, unpaid, overdue orders, open quotes, average order, revenue by month with a chart, and revenue by buyer

DOCUMENTS (Word + PDF)
• Wholesale Terms & Policies: minimums, pricing, payment terms, production and shipping, damages and shortages, minimum advertised price, online sales, exclusivity, resale certificates
• Wholesale Buyer Application: store info, resale certificate, payment and Net terms request, trade references, signature
• Printable Wholesale Order Form for trade shows and buyers who prefer paper
