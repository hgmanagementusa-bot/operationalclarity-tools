Tattoo Consent Form Kit

Quote it, book it, get the consent form signed and keep every record in one place: a complete tattoo consent form kit with a working pricing calculator and booking tracker, not just a single printable page.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- Tattoo Consent & Release Form (Word + PDF, 2 pages): client details, age and photo ID check (studio use), simple yes / no client self-disclosure questions, 9 initialed acknowledgments (permanence, design and spelling approved, results vary, risks, written aftercare received, deposit terms), release, photo consent, client and artist signatures.
- Tattoo Booking & Deposit Agreement (Word + PDF, 1 page, 10 sections): booking, deposit applied to the final session, price and payment (hourly or flat), rescheduling, cancellations and no-shows, late arrival, age and ID, on the day, artwork copyright and photos, touch-ups and refunds, signatures.
- Design Approval & Session Record (Word + PDF): design, size, placement and wording sign-off with initials, sketch / stencil box, session times, supplies with lot / batch numbers, payments and touch-up log.
- Studio Policies (Word + PDF): front-desk policies for deposits, rescheduling, age and ID, guests, pricing, designs, touch-ups and payments, plus a blank page for your studio's own aftercare instructions.
- Tattoo Pricing Calculator & Booking Tracker (Excel / Google Sheets, 6 tabs):
* Price Quote: tattoo hours x your rate + custom drawing time, never below your shop minimum, rounded quote, deposit, balance per session, plus artist share, supplies, card fees and studio net
* Quick price list from 0.5 to 6 hours with deposits
* Settings: rate, shop minimum, deposit % and minimum, artist share, supply cost per hour, card fees, minimum client age
* Clients: contact details, ID type checked, age, bookings and total spent (no health information stored)
* Bookings: deposits, refunds, status (booked / completed / rescheduled / no-show / cancelled), consent signed and ID checked flags, balances and alerts (UNDER MIN AGE / CONSENT MISSING / ID NOT CHECKED / BALANCE DUE / DEPOSIT DUE / UPCOMING)
* Dashboard: revenue, tips, studio net, deposits held, no-show rate, average ticket, revenue per tattoo hour, month-by-month and artist-by-artist tables, chart
- START HERE guide (PDF)

HOW IT WORKS
1. Set your rate, shop minimum and deposit rules on Settings.
2. Quote the piece in Price Quote and copy the price and deposit into the Booking & Deposit Agreement.
3. Take the deposit and add the booking.
4. On the day, check ID, have the client complete the Consent & Release Form and approve the design.
5. Record the session and watch the alerts for missing paperwork, balances and no-shows.
Example data (12 sample clients, 18 bookings) shows how it all connects; delete it and type your own. Yellow cells = type here, green cells = calculated.

FORMATS
