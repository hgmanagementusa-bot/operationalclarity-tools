Dance Studio Forms Kit

Get it on Etsy

Register dancers, get the waiver signed, set clear studio policies and track every tuition payment: a complete dance studio forms kit with a working tuition and enrollment tracker, not just Canva pages.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- Dance Student Registration Form (Word + PDF): dancer and parent details, emergency contacts and approved pick-up list, class selection with tuition, health and allergy notes for emergencies, how did you hear about us.
- Liability Waiver, Release and Photo / Video Release (Word + PDF): assumption of risk, release, emergency care, studio rules, personal property, photo/video permission (yes / no), communication consent, signatures, office-use box.
- Studio Policies & Parent Handbook (Word + PDF): season dates, tuition table, registration fee, sibling discount, payments and late fees, attendance and make-up classes, weather closures, dress code by style, studio etiquette, recital, withdrawal, signed acknowledgement.
- Dance Instructor Agreement (Word + PDF): 12-section independent contractor agreement - pay options, choreography fees, substitutes, child safety and background check, choreography ownership, non-solicitation, insurance, termination.
- Recital Costume Order Form (Word + PDF): measurements, costumes and fees, accessories, payment, office tracking.
- Recital Planning Checklist (Word + PDF): 20-week timeline from booking the venue to the thank-you email, plus a recital-day check-in sheet.
- Dance Studio Tuition & Enrollment Tracker (Excel / Google Sheets, 8 tabs):
* Settings: tuition tiers by hours per week, registration fee, costume fee and due date, sibling discount, late fee
* Classes: schedule, teacher, capacity; enrolled, spots left, fill % and FULL / ALMOST FULL alerts
* Students: up to 4 classes per dancer; weekly hours, tuition tier, sibling discount, monthly tuition, charges, paid, balance, overdue and status (PAID UP / DUE SOON / OVERDUE)
* Payments and Costumes logs (costume fees added to each dancer automatically)
* Roster: type a class ID and print the roster with an 8-week attendance grid
* Dashboard: active dancers, families, monthly tuition roll, collected, outstanding, overdue, class fill, month-by-month chart
- START HERE guide (PDF)

HOW IT WORKS
1. Type your tuition tiers, fees and class schedule in the spreadsheet.
2. Update the Policies & Parent Handbook with the same prices and send it with the Registration Form and Waiver.
3. Add each dancer with their classes: tuition, discounts and charges calculate.
4. Log payments and costumes, print rosters, and check the Dashboard for overdue accounts.
Example studio data (10 classes, 66 dancers) shows how it all connects; delete it and type your own. Yellow cells = type here, green cells = calculated.

FORMATS
- Word .docx (edit in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, Pages or LibreOffice) + PDF, US Letter
- Excel .xlsx (works in Excel and Google Sheets)
