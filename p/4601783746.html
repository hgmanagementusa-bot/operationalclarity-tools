Quote the project, sign it, control the revision rounds and get paid before you hand over the files: a complete graphic design contract kit with a working pricing calculator and project tracker, not just a Word file.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- Graphic Design Services Agreement (Word + PDF): 16 plain-English sections, 2 pages. Project and deliverables, fixed / hourly / retainer fees, rush fee, deposit and payment, late fee, revision rounds and extra round price, client responsibilities, proofs and approval, client materials, fonts and stock licenses, ownership and copyright transfer on full payment, source files, portfolio and credit, trademark note, confidentiality, independent contractor, cancellation and kill fee, limitation of liability, governing law, signatures.
- Design Proposal & Project Scope (Word + PDF): 3 packages (Starter / Standard / Complete), deliverables and file formats, milestones and payments, what's not included. Attach it to the agreement as Exhibit A.
- Creative Brief & Client Questionnaire (Word + PDF): goals, audience, competitors, style scale, colors and fonts, logo type, must-have text.
- Revision Request Form, Final Design Approval & Sign-Off, and Copyright Transfer (Assignment of Rights) (Word + PDF).
- Graphic Design Pricing Calculator & Project Tracker (Excel / Google Sheets, 9 tabs):
* Rate Planner: your minimum hourly rate from income goal, expenses, taxes and billable hours
* Quote Builder: hours per deliverable, contingency, rush fee, usage / licensing add-on, fonts and stock, rounded fixed fee, effective hourly rate, deposit, extra revision round price and a monthly retainer option
* Projects: revision rounds included vs used, extra revision fees, hours vs budget, due dates, invoiced, paid, balance, earned per hour, alerts (OVER BUDGET / EXTRA REVISIONS / DUE SOON / PAST DUE / 80% USED)
* Time Log and Expenses (billable Y/N, markup)
* Invoices: due dates, days overdue, late fee, PAID / OPEN / PARTIAL / OVERDUE status
* Printable Invoice: pick a project and dates, billable hours by task and expenses fill in
* Dashboard: invoiced, collected, outstanding, overdue, unbilled work, extra revision fees to bill, month-by-month chart
- START HERE guide (PDF)

HOW IT WORKS
1. Set your rate in the Rate Planner and your revision rules on Settings.
2. Send the Creative Brief, then price the job in the Quote Builder.
3. Send the Proposal; when the client says yes, fill in the Agreement, sign and invoice the deposit.
4. Log time, count revision rounds with the Revision Request Form, get the Final Approval signed, bill from the Invoice tab, and send the files and Copyright Transfer when paid in full.
Example data (4 sample clients) shows how it all connects; delete it and type your own. Yellow cells = type here, green cells = calculated.
