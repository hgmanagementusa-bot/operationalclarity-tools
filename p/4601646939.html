Mobile Bartending Contract Template Kit

Book the event, check the ID rules, plan the drinks and get paid: a complete mobile bartending contract kit with a working quote calculator, drink calculator and deposit tracker, not just a Word file.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- Mobile Bartending Services Agreement (Word + PDF): event details table + 15 plain-English sections, 2 pages. Services, alcohol supply and licensing (client-supplied or licensed bar), responsible service and ID checks, guest count and last call, pricing, deposit and payment, cancellation scale, overtime, client responsibilities (space, power, water, venue approval), gratuity and tip jar, insurance and liquor liability, equipment and damage, weather and safety, force majeure, governing law, signatures.
- Bar Package & Quote (Word + PDF): bar menu with signature cocktails and mocktails, pricing table, deposit and balance. Attach it to the agreement as Exhibit A.
- Final Details & Event Confirmation (Word + PDF): final 21+ / under-21 count, alcohol the client is supplying, event timeline, client sign-off.
- Mobile Bar Inquiry Form (Word + PDF): everything to ask on the first call.
- Alcohol & Bar Shopping List + Event Day Checklist + Refused Service & Incident Log (Word + PDF).
- Mobile Bar Quote, Drink Calculator & Event Tracker (Excel / Google Sheets, 8 tabs):
* Quote Builder: guests, hours, bar package, bartenders needed (auto from guests per bartender), barbacks, travel fee, extras, discount; quote total, price per guest, deposit, balance due date, headcount deadline, profit and margin
* Drink Calculator: total drinks, cases of beer, bottles of wine, bottles of each spirit, ice bags, mixer liters and cups, with estimated cost
* Events: deposit required, paid to date, balance, final payment and headcount due dates, margin, alerts (DEPOSIT DUE / BALANCE OVERDUE / GET HEADCOUNT / PAID IN FULL)
* Payments log and a printable Invoice
* Dashboard: booked revenue, collected, outstanding, overdue, average price per guest, margin, month-by-month chart
- START HERE guide (PDF)

HOW IT WORKS
1. Enter your rates, bar packages and travel fee on Settings.
2. Fill in the Inquiry Form on the first call, then price the event in the Quote Builder and plan the drinks in the Drink Calculator.
3. Send the Bar Package & Quote; when the client says yes, fill in and sign the Agreement.
4. Track the deposit and balance on Events and Payments, send the Final Details form before the deadline, and print the checklist and incident log for event day.
Example data (8 sample events) shows how it all connects; delete it and type your own. Yellow cells = type here, green cells = calculated.

FORMATS
- Word .docx (edit in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, Pages or LibreOffice) + PDF, US Letter
- Excel .xlsx (works in Excel and Google Sheets)
