Catering Contract Template Kit

Book the event, lock the guest count and get paid: a complete catering contract kit with a working quote calculator and deposit tracker, not just a Word file.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- Catering Services Agreement (Word + PDF): event details table + 15 plain-English sections, 2 pages. Services and menu, final guest count, per-guest pricing, service charge vs gratuity, deposit and payment, cancellation scale, client responsibilities, allergies and dietary needs, food safety and leftovers, alcohol, equipment and rentals, staff overtime, insurance and liability, force majeure, photos, signatures.
- Catering Proposal & Menu Quote (Word + PDF): menu by course with dietary labels, pricing table, deposit and balance. Attach it to the agreement as Exhibit A.
- Final Guest Count & Event Confirmation (Word + PDF): final numbers, guest allergies, event timeline, client sign-off.
- Catering Inquiry & Order Form (Word + PDF): everything to ask on the first call.
- Event Order (BEO) kitchen & staff sheet + Packing & Day-of Checklist (Word + PDF).
- Catering Quote Calculator & Event Tracker (Excel / Google Sheets, 8 tabs):
* Menu Costing: cost per serving from batch cost, suggested price at your target food cost %, your own price override
* Quote Builder: pick menu items and servings per guest, staff hours, rentals and delivery; service charge, tax, quote total, price per guest, deposit, balance due date, headcount deadline, profit after overhead
* Events: deposit required, paid to date, balance, final payment and headcount due dates, margin, alerts (DEPOSIT DUE / BALANCE OVERDUE / GET HEADCOUNT / PAID IN FULL)
* Payments log and a printable Invoice
* Dashboard: booked revenue, collected, outstanding, overdue, average price per guest, margin, month-by-month chart
- START HERE guide (PDF)

HOW IT WORKS
1. Enter your settings and cost your dishes on Menu Costing.
2. Fill in the Inquiry Form on the first call, then price the event in the Quote Builder.
3. Send the Proposal & Menu Quote; when the client says yes, fill in and sign the Agreement.
4. Track the deposit and balance on Events and Payments, send the Final Count form before the deadline, and print the BEO and checklist for event day.
Example data (8 sample events) shows how it all connects; delete it and type your own. Yellow cells = type here, green cells = calculated.

FORMATS

- Excel .xlsx (works in Excel and Google Sheets)
